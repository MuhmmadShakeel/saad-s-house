Our Story Since 1971
Since 1971, Saad’s Tailors Boutique has been creating men’s and women’s clothing that looks great, fits perfectly, and is made from the world’s best fabrics. Based in Houston, TX, our success comes from blending classic tailoring techniques with modern designs.

1971

Since our beginning in 1971, our goal has always been to create men’s & women’s clothing that looks great, fits perfectly, and is made from the world’s best fabrics and materials. Saad’s success is built on understanding the principles of classic tailoring, then combining them with modern designs.

A custom suit from Saad’s Tailors Boutique in Houston, TX, is more than just clothing—it’s an investment in your confidence and presence. Unlike off-the-rack options, a custom suit sets you apart with a polished, professional look that exudes the poise of a true gentleman. As soon as you put on your tailored suit, you’ll feel the difference in how you look and carry yourself, whether you’re at a Houston business event or a formal gathering.

The finest fabrics are the foundation of a perfect custom suit, and at Saad’s Tailors Boutique, we use only the best materials available. From premium wool to breathable blends, every detail is carefully selected to ensure your suit is as comfortable as it is stylish. Visit our Houston studio to explore our fabric options and see the difference for yourself.

3. Enjoy a Perfect Fit Tailored to Your Body

At Saad’s Tailors Boutique, we take pride in fitting your custom suit with precision and care. Our master tailors in Houston, TX, customize every stitch to match your body type, ensuring a flawless fit from head to toe. Unlike mass-produced suits, a custom suit offers enhanced comfort and style tailored specifically for you. Whether you’re in the Galleria area or beyond, we’ll make sure your suit fits like a second skin.

4. Handmade Quality by Houston’s Expert Tailors

Nothing beats the unmatched quality of a handmade suit, crafted with years of expertise. At Saad’s Tailors Boutique, your custom suit is made by hand in Houston, Texas, giving you a unique connection to your garment through a personal, local experience. Our skilled tailors bring decades of knowledge to every suit, ensuring exceptional craftsmanship that stands out. Visit our Houston tailoring studio to see the artistry behind each handmade suit.

5. Personalized Service with Room for Questions

When you choose a custom suit from Saad’s Tailors Boutique, you’ll enjoy personalized customer service that lets you collaborate on every detail. We encourage you to ask questions about styles, cuts, and fabrics so we can deliver exactly what you’re looking for. Our Houston team is here to provide feedback and guidance, ensuring your custom suit meets your vision. The Story of Saad Shaar: Houston’s Premier Custom Tailor at Saad’s Tailors Boutique

Saad Shaar, the founder of Saad’s Tailors Boutique , was born in Lebanon in 1947 and discovered his passion for fashion at a young age. By 11, Saad developed an eye for style, inspired by his father’s custom clothing. At 13, he boldly told his father he would one day become his tailor. Starting as an entry-level floor boy at his father’s tailor shop in Lebanon, Saad performed simple hems, eager to learn more. Frustrated by the lack of advancement to cutting and design, he left school to pursue his career in Lebanon’s capital, where he honed and mastered the art of tailoring. By the age of 20, as war broke out in Lebanon, Saad seized the opportunity to move to the United States, eventually settling in Houston, TX. There, he began working at a large department store, managing the alteration department, all while dreaming of opening his own clothing store.

Today, Saad’s Tailors Boutique is one of Houston’s first hand-tailored services for men and women, located at 5866 Westheimer Rd. in the Galleria area. We specialize in crafting the finest made-to-measure suits, tuxedos, sports jackets, slacks, and overcoats, all meticulously handcrafted by our expert tailors in Houston, Texas. Our garments are designed to meet the needs of innovative designers, specialty retailers, stylists, and individuals seeking custom clothing that reflects their unique style. Whether you’re looking for a custom suit for a wedding, business event, or special occasion in Houston, Saad’s Tailors Boutique delivers unparalleled quality and craftsmanship. Contact us at (713) 785-2212 to schedule a consultation and experience the art of custom tailoring!

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you visit our website, saadtailors.com , book an appointment, or use our services, including our online shopping cart (when available). By using our website, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

We collect the following types of information:

1.1 Personal Information

When you book an appointment or interact with our website, we may collect:

Name : To identify you for appointment scheduling.

Email Address : To send appointment confirmations and updates.

Phone Number : To contact you regarding your appointment.

Future Payment Information : If our shopping cart feature is enabled in the future, we may collect payment details such as credit card numbers, billing address, and other payment-related information to process transactions securely.

1.2 Non-Personal Information

We may collect non-identifiable information, such as:

Browser Information : Browser type, version, and settings.

Device Information : Device type, operating system, and IP address.

Usage Data : Pages visited, time spent on the site, and referral sources.

1.3 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience, analyze site usage, and improve our services. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

Appointment Scheduling : To manage and confirm your appointments at our Houston, TX location (5866 Westheimer Rd., Houston, TX 77057).

Communication : To send you appointment reminders, updates, and promotional offers via email or phone.

Future E-Commerce : If the shopping cart feature is enabled, we will use your payment information to process orders for custom suits, ready-made suits, and other apparel.

Website Improvement : To analyze usage data and improve our website’s functionality and user experience.

Legal Compliance : To comply with applicable laws, regulations, or legal requests.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information in the following cases:

Service Providers : With trusted third-party providers (e.g., email service providers, payment processors) who assist us in operating our website and services, subject to strict confidentiality agreements.

Legal Requirements : If required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, court order, or other legal process.

Business Transfers : In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or part of our business, your information may be transferred as part of the transaction.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. For future e-commerce transactions, we will use secure encryption (e.g., SSL) to protect payment information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

Access and Update : You can request access to or update your personal information by contacting us at info@saadtailors.com .

Opt-Out : You can opt out of promotional emails by clicking the “unsubscribe” link in our emails.

Brief info

Saad Shaar was born in Lebanon in 1947 ever since Saad was a young boy at the age of 11 he had an eye for fashion. His father always wore custom clothes and by the time Saad was 13 he told his father that one day he would be his tailor. He went to work for his father’s tailor as an entry-level floor boy performing simple hems. His boss would not advance him to cutting and design so, He made the decision to leave school and follow his career in the capital of Lebanon. It is there that he learned and mastered his craft by the time Saad was 20 the war in Lebanon had begun and he found an opportunity to leave to the United States. When he moved to Houston, TX he began working at a large department store managing the alteration department. Durning that time he had a dream of building his own clothing store.

In 1971 Saad found a hole in the wall business space in Montrose and negotiated a deal with the landlord that he would fix and repair the store if he would not charge him rent for 6 months. Saad opened his doors with only one sewing machine and little to no money. He then built Saad’s Tailors. His love for making men and women look their best spread around town and Saad’s Tailor began to flourish. Almost 50 years later and nothing makes Saad happier than to see the customers smile after his touches and alterations are made personally for them.

Saad is one of the first hand-tailored services for men and women in Houston. We construct garments to meet the requirements of innovative designers, specialty retailers, stylist, and individuals. We handcraft the finest made to measure suits, tuxedos, sports jackets, slacks, and overcoats crafted entirely by hand right here in Houston Texas.

Custom Suit Style Inspiration for Men

Get Ideas at Saad’s Tailors Boutique in Houston, TX

Finding the perfect style for a custom suit can be challenging, especially if you’re new to formal dressing or don’t often wear suits. At Saad’s Tailors Boutique in Houston, TX, we help men discover their ideal suit style with personalized tailoring services. Whether you’re inspired by athletes, musicians, politicians, or even your dad, popular culture offers plenty of ideas to guide your look. Let’s explore some suited-up professions and figures to inspire your next custom suit, and visit us at 5866 Westheimer Rd. in Houston’s Galleria area to bring your vision to life!

Athletes: Bold and Creative Suit Styles

Athletes are known for their uniforms, but many shine off the field with stylish suits. NBA stars like LeBron James , Russell Westbrook , and James Harden embrace the league’s dress code with vibrant colors and unique patterns, perfect for men who want a bold, modern look. For a more classic style, take inspiration from tennis legend Roger Federer , named GQ’s Most Stylish Man in 2016 . At Saad’s Tailors Boutique in Houston, we can craft a custom suit that captures your favorite athlete’s flair, tailored to fit you perfectly.

Musicians: Slim Fits That Stand Out

While many musicians opt for casual fashion, some are known for their sharp suit game. Artists like P. Diddy, Pharrell Williams , and Jack White often choose slim-fit suits that make a statement on stage or at award shows. Their sleek, tailored looks are ideal for Houston men who want to stand out at events or parties. Visit Saad’s Tailors Boutique in the Galleria area to create a custom slim-fit suit inspired by your favorite musician, ensuring you look as good as you feel.

Politicians: Classic Suits for a Confident Image

Politicians wear suits almost daily, making them a great source of inspiration for a professional, timeless look. If you’re aiming to project confidence and poise—whether for a business meeting or a community event in Houston—a traditional-fit suit in understated colors like navy, grey, or black is the way to go. Add a red, blue, or yellow tie to complete the look, perfect for your next big speech or presentation. At Saad’s Tailors Boutique, our expert tailors in Houston, TX, can craft a custom suit that gives you the polished image you need to succeed.

Your Dad: Modernize a Classic Look

Your dad might have been the first person you saw in a suit, maybe even teaching you how to wear your first one. Even if his suit is a bit outdated or snug, his classic style can inspire a fresh, modern update. At Saad’s Tailors Boutique in Houston, we can help you pay tribute to your dad’s timeless look with a custom suit that blends vintage charm with contemporary fit and fabric. Bring your ideas to our Houston tailoring studio, and let us create a suit that honors your family’s style legacy.

Ready to design a custom suit inspired by these iconic styles? Contact us  to schedule a consultation. Our master tailors in the Galleria area are here to help you find the perfect suit style for any occasion!
